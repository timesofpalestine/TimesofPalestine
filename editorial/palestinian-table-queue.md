# The Palestinian Table — المائدة الفلسطينية
## The weekly restaurant queue (owner directive 2026-08-04)

One famous Palestinian restaurant in the world, featured every week —
deep research, rights-cleared pictures, the restaurant's story, and its
most famous dishes WITH recipes. The daily editor cycle writes the next
entry every FRIDAY (both languages, slug
`originals/palestinian-table-<restaurant>-<year>.*`), moves it to
"Published" below, and adds at least one new candidate to keep the queue
full. Rotate regions: US → Europe → Arab cities → Latin America.

Rules: restaurant facts verified against current sources at writing time
(hours/locations change — phrase as verify-at-source); traditional
recipes written in our own words as common heritage; a chef's signature
dish is DESCRIBED with attribution to their cookbook or interviews,
never copied; journalism, not advertising — the story is the people and
the food's Palestinian roots.
