The American front pages, read from Palestine. Saturday, 5 September: a postwar strategy taking shape, a water crisis crossing the Gaza-Israel line, and an Israeli prime minister's campaign video naming Mahmoud Abbas alongside Iran's supreme leader.

## The scoop

Axios reported the Trump administration is drafting a postwar Middle East strategy built on containing Iran and expanding Israeli normalization with its neighbors — with the unfinished second phase of the Gaza plan grouped alongside Israel-Syria and Israel-Lebanon files as regional "fallout" to close out, not a standalone track.

## The story with a Palestinian stake nobody in Washington is naming

The Washington Post traced Israel's desalination-plant shutdown — five of six offline, 80 percent of supply cut — to an algae bloom Israeli experts partly blame on untreated sewage pouring from Gaza's war-destroyed treatment plants, while Israel's water utility has simultaneously halted the flow of water into Gaza itself.

## The campaign ad Washington is talking about

The New York Times covered Netanyahu's AI-generated video pairing Mamdani, Erdoğan, Khamenei and Abbas as gleeful enemies awaiting his election defeat — a fictional device the Times reported without finding any factual link between the four figures it names.

## Elsewhere in the capital

The Pentagon's leak-hunt polygraph tests of roughly 50 joint-staff officers, reported by the Times and Axios, and diesel's record $5.85-a-gallon price tied to the Iran war, both featured prominently — reminders of how much of Washington's current bandwidth the war with Iran is consuming at the Gaza file's expense.
