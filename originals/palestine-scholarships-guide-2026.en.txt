More than a hundred universities in at least twenty-five countries currently fund Palestinian students — bachelor's, master's and doctoral, in every field from medicine to music. No single Palestinian front page has kept that map in one place, updated, in both languages. This guide is that page.

The community database Academic Solidarity with Palestine alone lists around one hundred and fifty scholarship schemes. The British Council's HESPAL program has sent two hundred and fifty-six Palestinian scholars to British universities — one hundred ninety-six master's degrees and sixty PhDs — and places them across thirteen UK institutions at once.

Times of Palestine keeps this guide alive as a standing service: deadlines refreshed as they move, new programs added as they open, dead ones retired. Verify every deadline on the program's own page before planning around it, because windows shift year to year.

## Open right now — checked 29 August 2026

Two windows decide the next fortnight for anyone applying this autumn.

**Chevening closes on 6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC.** The 2027/28 round opened on 4 August, and the UK government's own timeline puts eligibility checks in October, shortlists in mid-February 2027 and interviews in March and April. It needs two years of work experience after the first degree, so the people it fits are already in jobs and already short of evenings.

**DAAD's in-country postgraduate window closes on 31 August 2026** — Monday. Anyone in the West Bank who has been assembling that file has the weekend.

Türkiye Bursları opens each winter and has not published its 2027 dates; the YTB–Baykar Palestine programme of 200 places sits under the same roof and has not published a window either. Both are worth a bookmark rather than a wait.

## Where to hunt beyond this page

Three community-run databases go deeper than any single article can: [Academic Solidarity with Palestine](https://academicsolidaritywithpalestine.org/) (the largest scheme-by-scheme list), the [Friends of Palestinian Universities student hub](https://friendsofpalunis.org/student-resource-hub/), and [Dutch Scholars for Palestine](https://www.dutchscholarsforpalestine.nl/scholarships-and-fellowships-palestine). The [Bseisu Foundation](https://www.bseisufoundation.org/scholarships) maintains its own fund and pointers to others.

A practical word from this desk to every applicant: the most common reason qualified Palestinians lose these awards is the calendar, not the competition. The flagship cycles cluster between August and February, reference letters take weeks to secure under the best conditions, and Gaza's connectivity makes everything slower.

Start the file months early, apply to several programs in parallel, and treat every deadline on this page as a date to verify at the source. This guide will be here, current, in both languages, every time you check back.
