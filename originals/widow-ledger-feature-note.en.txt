Times of Palestine has published a special bilingual investigation, [The Widow and the Ledger](https://timesofpalestine.com/suha-arafat/index-en.html), examining how an unsourced number attached to Suha and Zahwa Arafat became a fact everybody knows — and what the fabrication cost Palestinians.

In January 2025, the official account of the State of Israel posted four assertions about Zahwa Arafat, a private citizen who has never held office or been charged with an offence anywhere: that she is worth eight billion dollars, owns prime London real estate, lives in Paris, and qualifies for UNRWA funds as a refugee. The investigation tests each assertion against the public record. No filing, registry entry, audit or named source has ever been produced for the fortune or the property; she has lived in Malta, not Paris, since childhood, a fact fixed in wire photo captions and fifteen years of on-the-record interviews; and no evidence has been offered that she ever received anything from UNRWA.

The piece does not spare the documented record. It sets out what Palestinian and international auditors actually established about public money under Yasser Arafat — the roughly nine hundred million dollars in Authority revenue routed into a special account between 1995 and 2000, and the secret investment portfolio that reformist finance minister Salam Fayyad brought under public control and published. The argument is that a real accountability failure was buried under a fabricated one, and that Palestinians paid for both.

The full feature runs with five original infographics in a dedicated ledger-styled design, in [English](https://timesofpalestine.com/suha-arafat/index-en.html) and in [Arabic](https://timesofpalestine.com/suha-arafat/index-ar.html), and closes with a standing commitment to carry any response from those named, unedited.
