What the main Israeli dailies led with on Saturday, 5 September.

## Ynet

Ynet's top story followed Iranian reports of explosions near Kharg Island, Iran's main oil export terminal, after Donald Trump's repeated threats to strike it — alongside its own report that a failed attempt to reach the besieged Abu Rida home in Qusra "will explode in our faces," and coverage of US ambassador Mike Huckabee's visit to Turmus Ayya following a swastika-graffiti attack that targeted him.

## Maariv

Maariv's front page carried the Kharg Island reports and Huckabee's visit under the headline "terror is terror — and there will be consequences," alongside breaking coverage of a Lebanese Health Ministry claim that Israeli strikes killed four and wounded 32 overnight, and a report that Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner had landed in Moscow.

## Haaretz

Haaretz led its politics coverage with Jafar Farah's withdrawal from the Hadash-led joint list's Knesset slate after seven women brought harassment allegations to the party, and reported the Education Ministry's reversal of a school-transport cancellation affecting children in unrecognized Naqab villages after public pressure.

## The Jerusalem Post

The Post's lead poll story put the governing coalition at 51 Knesset seats — its strongest showing since May — after the Smotrich-Feiglin merger and Yechiel "Winter" 's new list, alongside a finding that 40 percent of Israelis believe there was "betrayal from within" on 7 October. Axios reporting on a Trump-administration post-war strategy for Iran and Israel also featured prominently.
