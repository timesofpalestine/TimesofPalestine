# Photographs — what to license, and why these ones

**I have not supplied any photographs.** Every usable image of Suha or Zahwa Arafat is owned by AFP/Getty, AP or Reuters. Publishing them without a licence would hand the subjects of this article — and the agencies — a straightforward legal complaint, in a piece whose entire argument is about evidentiary discipline. Licence them or run the piece with the figures alone.

The figures are designed to carry the article without photography. If budget is tight, run it with no photos at all rather than with unlicensed ones.

## Priority images — these are evidence, not decoration

The article's central checkable claim is that Zahwa Arafat lives in Malta, not Paris. These photographs are the proof. Licensing them turns an assertion into an exhibit.

## An editorial caution on image 3

That photograph includes Zahwa Arafat. Section VII of this article argues that she is a private person who should never have been dragged into any of this. Running her photograph — even sympathetically — puts the piece in tension with its own argument, and it hands her face to exactly the accounts that have been circulating it.

My recommendation: **run images 1, 2 and 4. Leave Zahwa out of the visual package entirely.** Let the section about her be text. The restraint is itself part of the argument, and readers will notice it.

If the editor decides otherwise, that is a legitimate call — but make it deliberately, and record the reasoning.

## What not to do

- **Do not screenshot the January 2025 post and publish the image.** Describe it, quote the four assertions, cite the date and the account. Reproducing the post amplifies it and imports whatever imagery it carried. Figure 5 already does this job properly.
- **Do not use any image sourced from a "net worth" page, a content farm, or a social post.** Several of those images are miscaptioned or are not the person at all. Using one would be the exact failure this article is about.
- **Do not use AI-generated portraits of either woman.** Under any circumstances.

## Licensing routes

- AP Images — `apimages.com`, editorial one-time web licence
- Reuters Pictures — `reuters.com/tools/pictures`
- Getty/AFP — `gettyimages.com`, editorial licence; confirm territory restrictions on the 2011 frame

Editorial web rates for a single-use, non-cover licence are typically modest. Budget for three images.

## Alternative visual package, no licensing required

If you publish without photography:

1. Open with **Figure 5** as the lead visual, full-bleed. It is the artifact under examination and it works as a hero.
2. Use the five SVGs in `/figures/` as social cards — `fig1` and `fig5` are the two that travel best.
3. Set the pull quotes ("The money went where the control was...") as standalone typographic cards.

This is a defensible and distinctive look for an accountability piece. Documents and ledgers, not faces.
